## R1 — P2: Critical activation and attribution gaps are not blocking

Locations in `scripts/p09_t01_1_flicker_suite.py`: lines 669–678 (Hit and Run),
603–613 (attribution), 930–933 (speed input), 969–974 (method blockers).

The seed's Flicker speed includes Hit and Run's 40% more skill speed. The
protocol states that the support requires 10 m movement before reuse, that
whether Flicker's teleport satisfies it is unconfirmed, and that the method
adds no separate movement time. Yet the unresolved activation rule is only
`informational` and absent from the method's blocking prerequisites.
Likewise damage attribution is still awaiting acceptance but is only
`ranking_sensitive`. D07 requires missing critical use/sustain mechanics and
ambiguous damage ownership to block a supported score. Its PoB-damage
amendment explicitly retains that rule.

Required repair: make unresolved activation and attribution prerequisites
blocking for affected evaluations. Resolve Hit and Run through evidence or
declare a separately identified conditional model; otherwise disable its
benefit or model the necessary movement and its time. Resolve the attribution
rule before a result can count as the supported objective. Keep the generated
gap records, method prerequisites, protocol and tests consistent.

Acceptance: resolving all other blocking gaps must not make this benchmark
eligible while its activation/attribution rule is still unresolved. Include a
case where movement has not re-enabled the skill and an attribution case with
an unassigned secondary damage source. No repeat-use or damage may be assumed
free because the gap is labelled low severity.

No score is computed today, so this is a defect in the implementation contract
for the next engineer, not a claim that this PR already emitted a false DPS.
