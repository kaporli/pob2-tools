## Findings

### R1 — P1: An unterminated reminder silently removes real modifiers

Location: `pob2-optimizer/pob2opt/parsers/gametext.py:203–211`.
The reader skips every following line in a section once a line opens a
parenthesis, without checking that the reminder ever closes. Insert
`(unterminated reminder` immediately before `+21 to Dexterity` in the ring
fixture: the Dexterity modifier disappears, no issue is reported, the item
and parse result are complete, and `export_item` reports a complete export.
Keeping raw source text does not repair the falsely complete normalized item.

Required repair: validate reminder boundaries. An unclosed or otherwise
uninterpretable block must refuse the input or preserve it as unresolved and
mark the item partial; it must not certify the reduced modifier list.
Acceptance: the reproduced malformed block cannot produce a complete item or
recommended export; valid single-line and multiline reminder text still works.

### R2 — P2: Impossible capture dates become validated listing observations

Location: `pob2-optimizer/pob2opt/market/capture.py:117–125`.
The timestamp regex checks shape only. Both `2026-02-30T18:00:00Z` and
`2026-13-16T25:00:00Z` produce complete imports, and all resulting Listing
records pass domain validation. Those are not RFC 3339 timestamps and cannot
support observation ordering or later freshness/refresh calculations.

Required repair: parse calendar/time values in addition to checking the
declared UTC representation, and reject them through the typed capture issue
path. Cover the top-level timestamp, per-entry override and `listed_at`.
Acceptance: impossible dates/times return `invalid_timestamp`, produce no
listings and exit through the CLI's invalid-input path. Include valid leap-day
and fractional-second controls; do not replace a rejected timestamp with now.
