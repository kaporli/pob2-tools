0.5.5c Patch Notes

0.5.5c Patch Notes


  • Fixed a rare issue where some players were unable to complete the Legacy of the Precursors questline after defeating the Arbiter of Divinity. You now have to have been present when the bosses of the Patriarch Halls and Matriarch Halls die in order to pick up the Origin Spark and Origin Cradle.
  • You now have to have been present when the bosses of the Western Enigma Chamber and Eastern Enigma Chamber die in order to pick up the Ancient Crisis Fragment and Weathered Crisis Fragment. Players that had already defeated the Arbiter of Ash but hadn't actually defeated Count Geonor and Doryani in the Enigma Chambers would have been unable to progress their Legacy of the Precursors Quest. Those players can now defeat the Arbiter of Ash and should be able to see and speak to Doryani after ascending the elevator in The Burning Monolith to progress their quest.
  • In order to access Act 4 Islands, you must now have turned in the Boat Charter to Makoru. Existing characters that did not do this already will be unaffected.
  • Fixed a bug where Deferred Items in Ritual could update their costs multiple times in a single area. This could happen each time Favours were Rerolled.
  • Fixed an issue where The Hooded One wasn't visible near Rog's shop in Kingsmarch if you had travelled to islands without having sailed to the island.
  • Fixed a bug where the player did not wake up in bed upon first entering the Ziggurat Refuge endgame town.
  • Fixed an issue where characters that either migrated from SSF or died in Hardcore didn't retain some Runesmithing Knowledge unlocks from the Runeseeker quests in the campaign.
  • Fixed a bug where Tavakai, the Chieftain could get blocked by player minions when moving to phase 2 of the fight.
  • Fixed a bug where the 2nd tier of the Trial of Chaos modifier "Wager of Chaos" was only giving +1 minimum rewards instead of +2.
  • Fixed a bug where Rakkar, the Frozen Talon could become immune to some damage if you parried it during certain skills.
  • Fixed a bug where Tor Gul, the Defiler could stop performing actions if you parried or stunned it during certain skill.
  • Added an "Identify Items" option to Balbala in the Trial of the Sekhemas.
  • Fixed a bug where players could get inside terrain in the Trial of Chaos.
  • Fixed an issue where Asinia and Draven could output the wrong names to chat when speaking out loud in the Forbidden Rites league.
  • Fixed a bug where Farrow's shop could fail to find Verisium stored in Expedition Stash Tabs.
  • Fixed 3 client crashes.
  • Fixed 3 instance crashes.
