## Implemented behavior

Before: every exported `SlotRule` had an empty category list and the blocking gap `gap/p02-t02/slot-accepted-base-categories`. The slot check and hand check answered `unknown`; pools placed only owned items in their source slots, so real imported gear stayed unplaced.

After: the exporter asks the engine. A new `slotAcceptance` rule source calls `ItemsTab:IsItemValidForSlot` for one probe item per base (1756) at normal, unique and relic rarity, against all 113 slots, and against both off-hand slots per main-hand base and keystone flag. Slot rules carry the categories the engine accepts for every base of the category, `refused_rarities` where a slot refuses unique or relic items, and `weapon_sets.off_hand_rules` per main-hand category and flag. Legality decides slot, rarity and off-hand cases; pools place real items by category and rarity. Anything a category or rarity list cannot express becomes a gap rather than a widened rule.

## Adverse cases

Exercised: a ring in the amulet slot (violation); a shield next to a quarterstaff (violation); a one-hand mace next to a two-hand mace (unknown, needs Giant's Blood); a unique jewel in a refusing tree socket (violation) and in a plain socket (legal); a jewel socket on an item (unknown, cites `socketed-item-jewel-slots`, not the P02-T02 gap); a category accepted for only some bases (empty rule plus blocking gap); a main-hand category whose bases disagree (no rule plus gap); a rarity refused for only some categories (no refusal plus gap); a profile without the probe (old empty rules and gap); `owned_from_build` without an origin (TypeError) or with an unknown one (ValueError).

Found by the engine cross-check: the first, categories-only version accepted a unique jewel in seven tree sockets the engine refuses. The rarity probe was added for that.

Untested or not modelled: the parent-item jewel-socket condition (`canSocketJewelBase`); the build's keystone flag state (pairings that need one stay `unknown`); jewel sockets of tree versions other than the default one (the probe runs on a blank build of the default tree; the cross-check skips any socket the probe did not see).

## Deviations and unresolved questions

- Base category changes from `type` to `type/subType` when a subtype exists. Needed because the engine splits flasks by subtype. One synthetic pool test changed its category string to match (`Two Handed Axe/Axe`).
- `owned_from_build` now requires `origin`. The only other caller is the historical review probe in `docs/evidence/PR-14-17/`, which runs against the old reviewed heads.
- Two optional record fields and one record kind are added (`contracts.md`). Old profiles decode unchanged; their meaning (no rule) is kept.
- Parser `p04-t01.6` / importer `p05-t02.2`: `Unique ID:` (a per-copy identifier, dropped) and `Runic Ward:` (a property) were read as modifier lines, shifting the implicit block. New `ModifierParseStatus.stat_resolved`: no single affix explains the line, but its text is a catalog modifier line's and its value is within what all catalog lines of that text sum to. Reparse `.5` records.
- Worth disagreeing with: the P04 review (R2, followup F1) requires ambiguous and out-of-range lines to keep an item partial and its builds non-recommendable. That is kept: such items stay `partial`, legality still answers `unknown` for their construction. The pool now places a partial item when its base resolves and every line's stat is known (`stat_known`), marked `stat_known_partial`. This relaxes P05-T03's "partial items are not offered" for that class only; an item with an unresolved line or base is still never placed.
- The P02 row slice was regenerated with `--summary` pointed at scratch, so neither `P02/catalog-export.json` (P02-T02's record) nor `P02-T02.1/catalog-export.json` changed. Five offline tests that asserted the old empty-slot premise on the slice now assert the engine rules.

## PR #22 review R8 repair (2026-10-19)

Review: [PR-18-22](../reviews/PR-18-22.md) R8 and the partial-item policy
section. Repair head on `p02-t03.1-slot-rules` after `2f9a9f7`.

- Uncertainty is executable: `SlotRule` and `OffHandRule` gain optional
  `undecided_categories`, `undecided_rarities`, `undecided_bases`, `gap_ids`
  (and `undecided` on off-hand rules). Mixed main-hand contexts are now
  exported as undecided rules instead of being dropped. One function,
  `catalog/slotrules.py` `decide`, answers accepted, refused or undecided
  for legality and pools alike.
- Legality: an undecided combination is `unknown`
  (`slot_rule_undecided`, `off_hand_rule_undecided`) citing the rule's gap;
  definite combinations keep `legal` or `illegal`.
- Pools: undecided items are placed only as exploratory candidates.
  Exploratory candidates (`stat_known_partial`, `slot_rule_undecided`,
  `owned_slot_unverified`) all carry `exploratory`; a slot with only such
  candidates is the new status `exploratory_only`, not `populated`.
  `pool_report` counts complete and exploratory candidates separately and
  lists `populated_slots` and `exploratory_only_slots`.
- Tests: six reviewer-derived regressions (partial rarity, partial category,
  pool provenance and counters, mixed and partial off-hand contexts, probe
  failure) plus two updated cases; all eight fail on `2f9a9f7`
  ([negative control](../evidence/P02-T03.1/repairs/negative-control-2f9a9f7-py312.txt)).
  The native matrix test now also asserts the pinned probe leaves nothing
  undecided.
- Results: offline 1716 passed, 1 existing xfail, on 3.12.4 and 3.10.22.
  Native (engine lock, clean pin): slot matrix 4, legality 7, Flicker 4,
  catalog 15, P04 round trips 53, item conversion 11, golden 8 passed.
- Real pool: 35 slots `populated` (154 complete candidates), Amulet,
