## Implemented behavior

Before: the only equipment candidates came from the legacy `harvest.py`: nine weapon categories, eight listings each, exalted only, a 60 exalted cap and name dedup. There was no pool record: `RunRequest.item_pool_ids` was listed as naming "a P05-T03 record type that does not exist yet".

After (`pob2-optimizer/pob2opt/market/pools.py`):

- `build_pool(catalog, profile, items, listings, owned, queries, bounds, gaps, created_at)` returns one `EquipmentPool` record with an entry for every `SlotRule` in the patch profile: weapons and swap sets, armour, jewellery, jewel sockets, flasks and charms, as the catalog names them. Each slot combines owned items, imported listings and imported items without a listing (availability `unknown`). Unavailable listings and `partial` items are left out and counted.
- Slot fit is catalog-driven only: the item base's catalog `type` must be in the rule's `accepted_base_categories`. A rule without categories makes the slot `missing_category_rule`; imported items are not placed there by guess, only owned items a source build put in that slot (`owned_from_build`). The pinned P02 export names no category for any slot (gap `slot-accepted-base-categories`), so a pool over it today holds owned items only, every slot says why, and the pool cites the gap.
- Sampling bounds `listings_per_slot` (default 500) and `enabling_queries_per_run` (default 100), configurable through `sampling()`, held in the pool and in its identity, and given for `RunManifest.search_parameters` by `sampling_parameters()`. Past the listing bound, candidates are taken round-robin over (base, modifier signature) groups, where the signature is the item's modifier categories and texts with numbers abstracted. Order never depends on price or any value; each slot reports `considered_count`, `retained_count` and `truncated`.
- Enabling declarations (`EnablingQuery`: bases and modifier regular expressions, optionally per slot) keep every matching item in the slots they apply to, past the bound and whatever its standalone value. No approved route can run them as market queries (D01), so inside the bound they are `unexecuted` with their local matches listed; past it they are `over_bound` and not applied. `pool_report()` gives truncated, empty and missing-category slots and query coverage (declared, unexecuted, over bound, executed 0, locally matched).
- No price, budget or score is held or taken: `build_pool` has no such parameter and the record no such field. Pairs and full configurations are P08's.
- `equipment_conflicts(build, listings, owned, pool)`: one listing fills at most `quantity` slots (unknown counts as one), an owned item at most its owned count, an item with neither at most one slot; with a pool, each slot's item must be one of that slot's candidates.
- `change_equipment(build, slot, candidate, profile, catalog, items, measure)`: replaces one slot, recomputes the build hash and runs the P02-T03 validator over the whole build. Attributes and resources depend on all equipment, so a measurement is taken of the new build through `measure`; without it those checks are `unknown`, never carried over.

New record kind `equipment_pool` (contract addition, below): dataclasses and enums in `domain/records.py` and `domain/enums.py`, generated schema `schemas/v1/equipment_pool.json`, `pool_identity`/`pool_hash` in `domain/identity.py`, semantic checks (slot status agrees with its rule, truncation agrees with the counts, an owned candidate names no listing, no duplicate candidates, no more in-bound queries than the bound) and reference resolution (catalog, profile, candidate items and listings, matched and unplaced items, gaps) in `domain/validation.py`, storage as a normalized record.

Not changed: `harvest.py`, `phases.py` and `policy.py` remain the legacy comparison path; nothing new calls them. Catalog, release and compatibility code is untouched.

## Deviations and unresolved questions

- Contract addition: the `EquipmentPool` record kind (`contracts.md`, `schemas/v1/equipment_pool.json`), which `RunRequest.item_pool_ids` names. No existing record or hash changed. Validation does not recompute `pool_id` from content, matching how item and build hashes are treated; `build_pool` derives it.
- Pools are populated only where a slot rule names categories. The accepted catalog names none, so real pools contain owned items only until P02-T02.1 or a catalog follow-up supplies slot categories. This task does not fill them in.
- Partial items are not offered as candidates. Game-text uniques import partial (P05-T02), so they are left out until their flavour text can be told from modifiers.
- Imported items without a listing count toward the per-slot bound with the listings; owned items do not.
- Enabling declarations match locally on modifier text and bases; there is no special-effect catalog yet, and none of the declarations here describes a real interaction.
- P05-T03 depends on P05-T02, which is not yet accepted.
