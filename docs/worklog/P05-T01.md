## Implemented behavior

No application behaviour changed. The deliverable is a written disposition.

Input: the published Path of Exile developer documentation and terms, the two
existing transport modules in `pob2-mcp`, and the specification's access
findings.

Previous behaviour: D01 was open. `pob2-mcp/pob2mcp/trade.py` and `poe_api.py`
existed as the only transport, with no recorded judgement on whether either is
a permitted route. `pob2opt` has no `market/` package and no provider.

Resulting behaviour: D01 is proposed with two separate dispositions.

- Route A, account-character import: supported subject to a named registration
  condition. The endpoint and scope are documented and correct. We hold no
  `client_id` of our own and new application registration is closed, so the
  route is approved in principle and unavailable in practice.
- Route B, public market acquisition: `unsupported_access`. No documented
  listing endpoint exists for PoE 2 or any realm, no scope could authorise one,
  and the undocumented website endpoints the current client uses are prohibited.

The record also fixes what `pob2opt market refresh` must emit for the
unsupported case, so P05-T04 implements a specified failure rather than
inventing one.
